CA Tracker UI Review

CA Tracker does the hard part well. It reads Helix and Procore in the background, matches every item to its CA folder, and puts a preview in front of every folder move. The screen doesn't show that off. It's laid out around how the tool works (sync, links, folder reconciliation) when people open it for three things: what's on my plate, what's late, and opening the document. Coordinators can't ask their basic question at all.

Findings

Critical blocks a core job or invites the wrong action High slows every session Medium consistency and polish

Triangles are revision deltas: the mockups below say which ones they fix.

1

There is no "my work" view Critical

The app records who you are: Helix gives your full name at sign-in, in the same form as the ball-in-court names, and Connections shows it as "Signed in as…". The item list never uses it. Every session starts on a single project with Ball in court set to "Everyone". Seeing your own items across projects depends on Ctrl-click, explained in small grey text at the bottom of the project list.

ViewModels/MainViewModel.cs:528 (PersonFilterText → "Everyone") · :884 ("Signed in as") · Scripts/bridge-core.js:265 (currentUser → fullName) · Views/MainWindow.xaml:172
FixA "My work" entry at the top of the project list, selected on first launch: all tracked projects, ball in court = the signed-in name, grouped by urgency. "Overdue, all projects" sits under it.
2

Coordinators can't choose people per project Critical

The Ball in court checks are one set of names applied to every project on screen. Checking Raj for Riverside also shows Raj's items on Northgate, where you don't manage him. The list offers only people who hold something right now, so you can't add a team member until they're given an item. Someone checked earlier who holds nothing here shows greyed as "(nothing here)", still filtering.

ViewModels/MainViewModel.cs:76 (rememberedPeople, one HashSet) · :3166 MatchesPerson · :3260 RebuildPersonOptions (current holders only)
FixWatchlists: saved sets of project → people. Pick from everyone who holds an item or is next on one (current and upcoming holders are both in the synced data). A people × projects board shows the counts.
3

The loudest button moves folders, and it's offered for open items Critical

The only accent-coloured button in the action bar is "Move N to Returned…". N counts every checked row whose folder is in the active folder, whether or not the item is Ready to return. In the running window, five open items in your court (three of them overdue) were checked and the bar offered Move 4 to Returned… in bright blue. The preview stops an accident, but people check those rows to download them, and the screen tells them filing is the main thing to do.

Core/Comparison/ProjectComparer.cs:56 (CanMarkReturned ⇔ folder is active) · Views/MainWindow.xaml:735
FixThe main button follows what's checked: Open, then Download. Moving to Returned lives in a Filing view and counts only rows that are Ready to return; anything else needs an explicit "also move items still in court" choice inside the preview, which keeps its Cancel button.
4

"Download and open" never opens anything Critical

Download opens a second window, reads a hidden Helix page, lists the files, saves them, and ends at an "Open folder" link that starts Explorer. Nothing opens the file itself. "Open Online" does two different things: Helix items open in a side panel, Procore items in your web browser. Double-clicking a row opens the folder, or the item when there's no folder, and only a tooltip on the Folder column says so.

Views/DownloadWindow.xaml.cs:331 (only explorer.exe) · Views/MainWindow.xaml:583 · Views/MainWindow.xaml.cs:1056
FixOne Open ▾ split button per row. The click opens the newest file (downloading it into the item folder first if it's missing). The menu has Open folder, Open in Helix/Procore, Download files… and Verify folder. Enter opens the selected row, Space checks it.
5

Overdue is a text colour in one column High

Only the "Days till due" text turns red. The rows look the same otherwise, there's no count above the list, and the date is hidden by default. The project-list badge ("2 overdue" then a bare "3") is the best urgency signal in the app, but nothing says what the 3 is, and both numbers change meaning silently when the filters change.

Core/Storage/ViewState.cs:48 (HiddenColumns = DueColumn) · Views/MainWindow.xaml:628, :280–292 · ViewModels/ViewModels.cs:410 (meaning only in a tooltip)
FixGroup by urgency by default (Overdue · Due today · Next 7 days · Later · No due date), a red or amber stripe on the row's left edge, and a summary strip whose numbers are filters. Badges say what they count: "3 late · 5 open".
6

Fourteen columns, several saying the same thing High

Title and Folder repeat each other: "000252 - BMS Panel…" appears twice on every row. Ball in court shows your name on every row once you've filtered to yourself. "Item status" (Review) sits next to "Folder status" (Open): two status columns, and the second one's "Open" pill says nothing. Discipline has a permanent "Set" link on every row for a rare setup task. The 268px action column repeats "Folder · Open Online · Download" in blue down the page, and when everything is a link nothing stands out.

Views/MainWindow.xaml:557–692 · running window, 7 rows
FixDefault columns: Due, Project (when several), Number, Title, With / Days with them, Open ▾. Ball in court hides itself when one person is filtered. Folder state shows only when something's wrong (no folder yet, ready to return, two folders). Discipline moves to the details pane and the right-click menu.
7

Seven filter controls, all the same weight High

Type tabs, Show, Ball in court, Due, Discipline, Group by and Sort by share one row that wraps onto two lines in the default 1280px window. "Show" mixes two questions: which items (Open, All) and folder housekeeping (Needs attention, Ready to return, No folder, In Returned folder). Group by and Sort by are view settings you set once, not filters, and clicking a column heading already sorts.

Views/MainWindow.xaml:403–527
FixOne row of chips: [You ▾] [RFIs] [Submittals] [+ Filter], with the urgency numbers above it. Grouping, sort and columns go behind a single View button. Housekeeping moves to the Filing view.
8

The project header is spent on setup High

The top of the screen shows the CA folder path, the Helix link, two sync timestamps, an orange "79 folders are hidden because their names say neither BB nor TFO" warning with a button that edits the project, and "Sync from Helix" as the brightest button on screen, for something that already runs every 15 minutes. Nothing up there says how the project is doing.

Views/MainWindow.xaml:316–400 · :320 (AccentButton) · :388
FixName, health ("3 late · next due Mon") and a quiet "Synced 2 min ago". CA folder, link and BB/TFO live in Edit project; setup problems collapse into one "⚠ 1 setup issue" chip that opens it.
9

Sync is reported in seven places High

The sign-in banner, Sync all ▾, Sync from Helix, every project's subtitle, the spinning mark beside each project, the progress card above the list and the footer's "Working…". For an app that syncs on its own this is noise that pushes the work down the screen.

FixOne sync pill in the top bar ("● Synced 2 min ago ▾", amber when something failed) with Sync now, per-site status and Connections in its menu. Keep the banner only for an expired sign-in that affects projects you track.
10

The project list mixes two lists and repeats sync times Medium

"No CA folder yet (8)" is longer than the tracked list above it. Every subtitle reads "BB · Helix · synced today 5:30 PM", the same text eight times. Multi-select by Ctrl-click is invisible and easy to lose.

FixSubtitles show health ("3 late · 5 open" or "next due Oct 20"). "No CA folder yet" starts collapsed. Hovering a project shows a checkbox to add it to the view.
11

Group headings repeat the page title Medium

With one project selected, the list opens with "Aligned CMH03 · 7" then "RFIs · 3", and the project name is already the heading above. The default "Project, then type" adds two heading levels for a single project.

FixGroup by urgency by default. Add a project level only when several projects are shown.
12

Useful features hide behind right-click and tooltips Medium

Choosing columns (right-click a heading), favourites and archive (right-click a project), multi-select (Ctrl-click), double-click to open the folder, F5 to sync: none of them are visible. The grid doesn't respond to Enter or Space.

FixA View button for columns, a visible ⋯ on project hover, checkboxes for multi-select, and Enter / Space / Ctrl+O on rows, listed in a "Keyboard shortcuts" item under Settings.
13

One checkbox does three unrelated jobs Medium

The row checkbox tooltip reads "Check to set its discipline, download it, or include its folder in a move". So the action bar always shows six buttons (Check all ready to return, Clear checks, Set discipline, Download, Move back, Move to Returned), and it stays on screen when nothing is checked.

Views/MainWindow.xaml:569, :704–739
FixThe bar appears only when something is checked and shows only actions that apply to those rows. Filing actions live in the Filing view.
14

The item panel is a website inside the app Medium

Helix items open as the full Helix page in a side panel; Procore items leave for your browser. It's slow to load, Helix-only, looks nothing like the rest of the app, and is already exposed to Helix moving pages to its new app.

FixA native details pane built from the synced data (status, due, who has it, who's next, attachments, folder) that works the same for both sites, with "Open in Helix / Procore" for anything else.
15

Wasted space and duplicated chrome Medium

The in-app "SMC CA Tracker" heading repeats the window title and costs a whole row. With seven rows, roughly 60% of the grid is empty while the useful detail sits in tooltips.

FixUse the top bar for search and the sync pill; use the right side for the details pane.
16

Labels come from the code Medium

"Folder status: Open", "Needs attention", "Move back to active…", "Link…", "Include them in BB…", "Not in last Procore view". Construction people know "ball in court", but an "Open" pill beside an item status of "Review" leaves them guessing.

FixSay what it means: "No folder yet", "Ready to return", "In Returned, but back with you", "Two folders match". Keep "checked" (never "ticked").

Keep these

  • Background sync with no windows, every 15 minutes
  • A preview with Cancel before any folder moves
  • Overdue badges in the project list
  • Filters, sort and selection remembered between sessions
  • Columns sized to their longest text, not just the rows on screen
  • The empty state that says why and offers "Clear filters"

Roadmap

Ordered so each step is useful on its own. Every count comes from rows already in memory, so none of this should slow the list.

Phase 1 · small XAML and view-model changes

Quick wins

  • Make Download the accent button; Move to Returned becomes secondary and counts only Ready to return rows △3

    CheckSummary and the action bar only; the move itself (Operations.cs) is untouched.

  • Default Ball in court to the signed-in name on first run △1

    store.Account(Helix).UserName is the Helix fullName, the same form as the holder names.

  • Show the due date by default and merge it with Days till due ("Sep 7 · 26 days late") △5
  • New group mode "Urgency", the default for one project; left-edge stripe on late and due-today rows △5△11
  • Hide the Folder column by default, Ball in court when one person is filtered, and the "Open" folder pill △6
  • Badges say "3 late · 5 open"; action bar hides when nothing is checked and drops buttons that don't apply △5△13
  • Enter opens, Space checks; drop the in-app title row △12△15

Phase 2 · new views on existing data

Built for the engineer

  • "My work" and "Overdue, all projects" at the top of the project list △1

    Every tracked project selected, with a preset person and due filter.

  • Open ▾ split button with "open newest file" △4

    Reuses HelixDownloadSession to fetch one attachment into the item folder, then opens it. Helix only; Procore rows open in Procore.

  • Native details pane in place of the Helix web panel △14

    Status, due, current and upcoming holders, attachment count; files load when an item is selected.

  • Chip filter row, View menu, one sync pill △7△9
  • Compact project header with health; setup issues in one chip △8△10

Phase 3 · coordinators

People across projects

  • Watchlists: project → people, chosen in a grid △2

    Stored in ViewState keyed by ProjectViewModel.ArchiveKey; MatchesPerson checks the row's own project. Choices come from current and upcoming holders.

  • Team board with drill-down and "Copy as list" △2△5

    Copies plain text to the clipboard; nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Filing view with tabs △3△16
  • Optional: share watchlists through the company server; a dot on items changed since you last looked (ModifiedAt)
Kept from the owner's rules: every folder move still opens a preview with Cancel first · the UI says "checked", never "ticked" · Procore is read only through the built-in browser (no API), so Procore rows open in Procore rather than listing files · nothing here adds disk or network work to showing the list.
